## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much CO2 does a typical website emit?

Estimates vary. Heavy, poorly cached pages can emit a gram or more of CO2 per page view. Well-cached static pages often measure in the low milligrams per visit.

### Why are static sites more eco-friendly?

Static sites are built once and served from cache. That reduces origin requests and per-request compute, which lowers energy use and carbon emissions.

### What is a sustainable website?

A sustainable site serves lean pages, minimizes unnecessary JavaScript, optimizes images, and uses caching and a CDN. Measure results with tools such as Website Carbon or WebPageTest.

### Can I self-host LightCMS and still keep the site green?

Yes. Running your own LightCMS instance does not change how visitors receive pages. The site still serves static content from your CDN, so hosting choices for the site and CDN determine most of the carbon footprint.
